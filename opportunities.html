Awareness & opportunities

Verified jobs, internships, scholarships and free learning resources. We check every listing on the official source and show the date we checked it.

Stay safe: genuine employers and scholarship schemes don’t ask you to pay a fee to apply, and we never charge for anything. If someone asks you for money in exchange for a job, don’t pay. Always apply through the official website.

Verified opportunities

Each listing links to the original source. Listings past their deadline are marked and moved to the end.

Free public resources

Official portals for scholarships, courses, jobs and internships. These are external websites; we are not connected with them.

What we share

  • Jobs and internships
  • Scholarships and educational opportunities
  • Free online courses
  • Government programmes and public resources
  • Resume preparation and interview skills
  • Digital literacy and professional development

How we check information

  1. Find it on the official sourceThe organisation’s own website or an official government portal.
  2. Confirm the detailsEligibility, location, deadline and how to apply.
  3. Record the date we checkedShown on every listing as “Verified”.
  4. Mark it when it closesExpired listings stay visible but are clearly labelled.
