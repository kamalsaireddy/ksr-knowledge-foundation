Book donation & redistribution

Books that helped you can help someone else. We collect donated books and pass them on, free, to students and job seekers who ask for them.

Donate booksRequest books

Why books

Preparation material is often the first cost a student can’t cover. A used grammar book or interview guide is still just as useful, and passing it on keeps it in use.

Who can donate

Anyone: individuals, families, teachers, colleges, libraries and bookshops. You don’t need to be a member.

Books we accept

  • English grammar books
  • Dictionaries
  • Vocabulary and spoken-English guides
  • Interview and aptitude preparation
  • Educational textbooks
  • General reading books

Please make sure books are readable, with no missing pages. We can’t accept heavily damaged or water-damaged books.

How a donation moves

Every step is recorded so we can report honestly on how many books came in and where they went, without sharing anyone’s personal details.

  1. You tell us what you haveUse the donation form. A rough description is enough.
  2. We review the booksWe check categories and condition, and sort them by subject and level.
  3. Handover is arranged privatelyCollection or drop-off, agreed by email or phone. We never publish addresses.
  4. Books are cataloguedEach batch is added to our internal record.
  5. Books reach a readerWe match books to requests, or share them at a distribution event, and record the handover.

Requesting books

Students, job seekers and anyone preparing for a goal can ask for books. Tell us what you’re preparing for and the language you prefer.

Requests are matched to what we have, so we can’t guarantee every request will be fulfilled. We’ll let you know either way.

Transparency

  • We never ask for money for book donations.
  • Donor and recipient details stay private.
  • Totals for books collected and distributed are published on our home page once recorded.
