English learning & communication

Practise speaking in a friendly group, build vocabulary and get comfortable with interview conversations. Every session is free.

Weekly speaking practice

Small groups, simple topics and a facilitator who keeps it relaxed. Mistakes are expected.

Interview communication

Practise introductions, common questions and mock interviews with feedback from volunteers.

Group discussions

Learn to share an opinion, agree and disagree politely, and take turns, the way placement GDs expect.

Vocabulary building

Useful everyday and workplace words, with examples you can use straight away.

Free materials and videos

Free English resources

What to expect

These sessions are designed to build confidence and support your learning. They don’t promise fluency or a job: progress depends on regular practice, and we’ll be honest with you about that.
