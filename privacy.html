Privacy policy

What information we collect, why, and how we look after it.

This is a starting template written in plain language. Before formal launch, have it reviewed by someone familiar with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Who we are

KSR Knowledge Foundation is an early-stage social-impact initiative. You can contact us at .

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms: usually your name, email, city or area, and the details of your request. Phone numbers are always optional. We never ask for Aadhaar numbers, bank details, passwords or other sensitive identity documents.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to you and coordinate the activity you asked about (an event, a book donation or request, volunteering).
  • To send updates, only if you chose to receive them.
  • To keep anonymous totals, such as the number of books distributed.

Where it goes

Form submissions are delivered to our email inbox through a form-processing service. They are not stored on this website, in its public code, or in your browser. We don’t sell or share your details with advertisers.

What we publish

We never publish the names, contact details or addresses of donors, recipients or members. We only publish photos, videos, quotes or stories of people with their clear permission, and for anyone under 18, with a parent or guardian’s permission.

Young people

If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before sending us your details. Sessions involving minors are held in public or supervised settings.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while you are part of the community or while your request is active, and delete them when they’re no longer needed or when you ask us to.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your details, or stop sending you messages, at any time by contacting us.

Videos

Embedded YouTube videos use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode. When you play one, YouTube’s own privacy policy applies.
